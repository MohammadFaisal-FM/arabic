## Vocab & Sarf notes

| Word | Type (ism / fi'l / harf) | Root | Meaning |
|------|--------------------------|------|---------|
| جاني | **fi'l** + pronoun | ج ي ء | it came to me (colloquial **جا**) |
| الهوى | **ism** | ه و ي | the wind / passion / longing |
| محملني | **ism** (active participle) | ح م ل | carrying to me / loaded with |
| رسالة | **ism** | ر س ل | a letter / a message |
| كتاب | **ism** | ك ت ب | writing / what is written |
| انكتب | **fi'l** (Form VII) | ك ت ب | it was written |
| وينك | **harf** + pronoun | — | where are you (**وين** = where) |
| عيوني | **ism** | ع ي ن | my eyes (term of endearment) |
| ارتوى | **fi'l** (Form VIII) | ر و ي | was quenched / drank its fill |
| تحرّك | **fi'l** (Form V) | ح ر ك | it moved / it stirred |
| شجون | **ism** | ش ج ن | longing / sorrow / deep feeling |
| تبعدوني | **fi'l** (Form IV) | ب ع د | you keep me away |
| زمن | **ism** | ز م ن | time |
| فرحة | **ism** | ف ر ح | joy |
| سنين | **ism** | س ن و | years |

**يا عيوني** is not literally “my eyeballs.” In Levantine/Iraqi talk it means *my dear* — the person you see with.

**الهوى** here is both the **wind** that carries the letter and **love/longing**. Folk songs often mean both at once.
