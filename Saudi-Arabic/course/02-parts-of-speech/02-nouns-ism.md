# Lesson 2.2 — Nouns (اسم / Ism)

## English → Arabic

| English | Arabic term |
|---------|-------------|
| Noun | **اسم** (ism) |
| Pronoun (I, you, he…) | **اسم** (ism) |
| Adjective (big, tired…) | **اسم** (ism) — not separate class |
| Adverb (sometimes) | often **ism** or **harf** phrase |

---

## Types of ism

| Type | English | Examples |
|------|---------|----------|
| Common noun | thing/place | بيت، مطبخ |
| Proper noun | name | محمد، رياض |
| Pronoun | I, you, he | أنا، أنت، هو |
| Relative pronoun | who, which | الذي |
| Demonstrative | this, that | هذا، هذه |

---

## Gender & number (preview)

| Feature | Arabic |
|---------|--------|
| Masculine / feminine | most nouns have gender |
| Singular / dual / plural | dual = exactly 2 (formal MSA) |

Najdi often uses **plural** patterns in speech; dual less common in dialect.
