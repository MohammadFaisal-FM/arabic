# Lesson 6.3 — Family Dialogue Scenarios

## Scenario bank (rotate weekly)

### 1. Calling a relative
**English:** Hi grandma, how are you? I'll visit Friday with the family.  
**Najdi starter:** السلام عليكم يا جدتي، شلونج؟ بجي يوم الجمعة مع أهلي.

### 2. Food at a gathering
**English:** The food is ready. Come eat.  
**Najdi:** الغدا جاهز. تعالوا كلو.

### 3. Yesterday's visit
**English:** Yesterday I went to my uncle's. We ate kabsa.  
**Najdi:** أمس رحت عند عمي. أكلنا كبسة.

### 4. Eid / visit plans
**English:** Will you come for Eid? We'll prepare lunch.  
**Najdi:** بتجون العيد؟ بنسوي غدا.

### 5. Checking on kids
**English:** How are the kids? Are they tired?  
**Najdi:** شلون العيال؟ تعبانين؟

---

## Practice method

1. Read English aloud  
2. Cover English — say Najdi from memory  
3. Record yourself — compare to model  
4. One scenario per **Friday review**
