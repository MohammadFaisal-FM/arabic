# Lesson 1.2 — Letters, Vowels, Connecting

## Short vowels (harakat)

| Mark | Name | Sound | English near |
|------|------|-------|--------------|
| َ | fatḥa | a | cat |
| ِ | kasra | i | sit |
| ٌ | ḍamma | u | put |

Example: **كَتَبَ** (kataba) = he wrote — three syllables with vowels shown.

In **books, signs, and WhatsApp**, vowels are usually **omitted**. You learn words by shape + context.

---

## Long vowels

| Letter combo | Sound |
|--------------|-------|
| ا | aa / aa |
| و | oo / uu |
| ي | ee / ii |

**كتب** (no vowels) = katab context; **كِتاب** (kitāb) = book.

---

## Definite article: ال

**ال** = "the" — attached to start of noun:

| Without | With ال | Meaning |
|---------|---------|---------|
| بيت | **ال**بيت | the house |
| كتاب | **ال**كتاب | the book |

Pronunciation: "al-" but often sounds like **a-** before sun letters.

---

## Sun & moon letters (brief)

After **ال**, some letters merge (sun letters): e.g. **ال** + **ش** → ash-shams (the sun).

Don't memorize the list yet — notice it when listening.
