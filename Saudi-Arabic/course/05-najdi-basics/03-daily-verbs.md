# Lesson 5.3 — Daily Verbs (Najdi)

## Motion

| Verb | Root | Past (he) | Najdi future | English |
|------|------|-----------|--------------|---------|
| go | ر-و-ح | راح | **ب**يروح | went / will go |
| come | ج-ي-ء | جا | **ب**جي | came / will come |
| return | ر-ج-ع | رجع | **ب**يرجع | returned / will return |
| visit | ز-ي-ر | زار | **ب**زور | visited / will visit |

## Daily actions

| Verb | Root | Past (he) | English |
|------|------|-----------|---------|
| eat | أ-ك-ل | أكل | ate |
| drink | ش-ر-ب | شرب | drank |
| sit/stay | ق-ع-د | قعد | sat / stayed |
| sit | ج-ل-س | جلس | sat (formal) |
| sleep | ن-و-م | نام | slept |
| make/do | س-و-ي | سوى | made / did |
| say | ق-و-ل | قال | said |

---

## Priority drill order

1. `drill verb قال` — fix قلت vs قالت  
2. `drill verb راح`  
3. `drill verb جا`
