## How this course works

Arabic is taught differently from English. This course follows the traditional **Sarf + Nahw** model, with every Arabic term explained in English.

| Phase | Modules | Goal |
|-------|---------|------|
| **Start** | 0 | Understand how Arabic differs from English |
| **Basics** | 1–2 | Read script; know the three word types |
| **Grammar** | 3–4 | Build words (Sarf) and sentences (Nahw) |
| **Najdi** | 5–6 | Speak with family — greetings to conversation |

**Time estimate:** 12–16 weeks at 45–60 min/day, 5 days/week.

## Weekly rhythm (after Module 2)

| Day | Activity | Command |
|-----|----------|---------|
| Mon | Root / word study | `drill root [root]` or `drill word [word]` |
| Tue | Verb deep-dive | `drill verb [verb]` |
| Wed | Listening (15 min) | YouTube / family audio |
| Thu | Passage | `passage` or `maqta` |
| Fri | Review + speak aloud | Re-read week; 5 sentences |
