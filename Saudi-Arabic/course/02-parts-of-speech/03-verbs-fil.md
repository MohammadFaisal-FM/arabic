# Lesson 2.3 — Verbs (فعل / Fi'l)

## English → Arabic

| English | Arabic |
|---------|--------|
| Verb | **فعل** (fi'l) |
| Past tense | **الماضي** (al-māḍī) |
| Present tense | **المضارع** (al-muḍāri') |
| Imperative | **الأمر** (al-amr) — command |

Arabic verbs carry **person, gender, and number** in the ending — English uses separate words (I, you, he).

---

## Example: ك-ت-ب (write)

| English | Arabic | Transliteration |
|---------|--------|-----------------|
| he wrote | كَتَبَ | kataba |
| he writes | يَكْتُبُ | yaktubu |
| I wrote | كَتَبْتُ | katabtu |
| write! (m.) | اُكْتُبْ | uktub |

---

## English speaker tip

| English | Arabic pattern |
|---------|----------------|
| "He **is** writing" | often just present verb: **يكتب** |
| "He **will** write" | **س**يكتب (MSA) or **ب**يكتب (Najdi) |

## Najdi high-frequency verbs

| Arabic | Root | English |
|--------|------|---------|
| أكل | أ-ك-ل | ate / eat |
| راح | ر-و-ح | went |
| جا | ج-ي-ء | came |
| قعد | ق-ع-د | sat / stayed |
| قال / قلت | ق-و-ل | said / I said |
| سوى | س-و-ي | made / did |
