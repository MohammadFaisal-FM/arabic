# Lesson 6.4 — Listening & Speaking Routine

## Wednesday routine (15 min)

1. **Watch once** — get the gist  
2. **Replay** — pause on 3 phrases  
3. **Write** new phrases in progress.md  
4. **Say each 3×** aloud
