# Lesson 1.3 — Reading Your First Words

## Goal

Read 10 common words without full vowel marks.

---

## Word list

| Arabic | Transliteration | English | Type |
|--------|-----------------|---------|------|
| أنا | anā | I | ism (pronoun) |
| أنت | anta | you (m.) | ism |
| هو | huwa | he | ism |
| هي | hiya | she | ism |
| في | fī | in | harf |
| من | min | from | harf |
| بيت | bayt | house | ism |
| كتاب | kitāb | book | ism |
| كتب | katab | he wrote | fi'l |
| أكل | akal | he ate | fi'l |

---

## Mini-sentences (MSA shape — Najdi comes later)

| Arabic | English |
|--------|---------|
| أنا في البيت | I am in the house |
| هو كتب | He wrote |
| هي أكلت | She ate |

---

## English speaker tip

Arabic **does not use "is"** in present tense the same way:

- **أنا في البيت** = literally "I in the house" = I am in the house

This is a **nominal sentence** (Module 4.2).
