# Lesson 3.3 — Masdar, Participles, Derived Nouns

## English mapping

| Arabic term | English term | Role | Example (ك-ت-ب) |
|-------------|--------------|------|-----------------|
| **مصدر** (maṣdar) | Verbal noun | the act of … | كتابة (writing) |
| **اسم فاعل** | Active participle | doer / -ing | كاتب (writer) |
| **اسم مفعول** | Passive participle | -ed / undergone | مكتوب (written) |
| Derived noun | — | place, tool, concept | مكتب (office), كتاب (book) |

---

## Example: أ-ك-ل

| Type | Arabic | English |
|------|--------|---------|
| Masdar | أكل | eating |
| Active participle | آكل | eater / eating |
| Passive participle | مأكول | eaten / food |
| Noun | أكلة | meal |

---

## Why this matters for Najdi

Family talk uses **participles** a lot:

- **الولد آكل** — the boy is eating
- **جدتي جالسة** — grandma is sitting
