# Lesson 4.4 — Negation & Questions

## Negation

| Type | MSA | Najdi (common) | English |
|------|-----|----------------|---------|
| General no/not | **لا** | **ما** / **la** | not / no |
| Never / not at all | **ليس** | **ما** + verb | is not |
| Don't (command) | **لا** + present | **لا** + verb | don't … |

| Arabic | English |
|--------|---------|
| ما أكل | he didn't eat (Najdi) |
| لا تروح | don't go |
| ما في | there isn't |

---

## Questions

| Method | Example | English |
|--------|---------|---------|
| Question word | **متى** بجي؟ | When will I come? |
| Question word | **وين**كم؟ | Where are you all? |
| Question word | **وش** / **إيش** | What? (Najdi) |
| Yes/no | **أ**كلت؟ | Did you eat? |
| **هل** (MSA) | هل أكلت؟ | Did you eat? |
