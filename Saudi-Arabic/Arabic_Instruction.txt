### Instructions for Learning Arabic / Najdi Dialect

These guidelines define how the assistant should handle user requests.

**Full framework (Sarf, Nahw, templates, dialect policy):**
See [Arabic-Learning-Framework.md](Arabic-Learning-Framework.md)

---
#### 0. Drill Word Exercise (Universal)
**Trigger:** `drill word [word]`

1. Classify the word: Root Family | Ism (اسم) | Harf (حرف)
2. Apply the matching template (A, B, or C) from the framework
3. Include brief dialect reference when relevant (Najdi primary)
4. Log in progress.md under drilled words

---
#### 1. Drill Root Exercise
**Trigger:** `drill root [root]`

**Focus:** Sarf

**Steps:**
1. Identify the root and give its base meaning
2. List common verb forms used in **spoken Najdi Dialect** and MSA
3. List masdar, active participle, passive participle, common derived nouns
4. Provide root family overview (ROOT → FORMS → VERB → MASDar → PARTICIPLES → DERIVED WORDS)

---
#### 2. Drill Verb Exercise
**Trigger:** `drill verb [verb]` (in Arabic or English)

* Identify verb form number
* Conjugate in: Present, Past, Future, Imperative
* Covers all **8 pronouns** in order: أنا, إحنا, أنت, أنتِ, أنتم, هو, هي, هم
* Presents in structured tables with: Najdi verb form, example sentences (Najdi Arabic, English, Transliteration)
* Also includes: **Masdar**, **Active Participle (اسم فاعل)**, **Passive Participle (اسم مفعول)**, derived forms, Nahw classification where relevant
* Use **new vocabulary** in family-context Najdi examples

---
#### 3. Passage / Maqta Exercise
**Trigger:** `passage` or `maqta`

* Generates a 3–4 sentence **English passage** with **Easy**, **Medium**, or **Hard** level (**randomized**)
* Marks **key MSA nouns and verbs in brackets with both english and arabic** within the passage
* Dont give the key MSA nouns and verbs separately only in the passage its enough as in the previous line commanded
* User translates into **Najdi Arabic**
* Assistant provides:
  * Correct Najdi translation with **transliteration**
  * **Corrections and improvement suggestions**
  * **Highlights new Najdi vocabulary**
  * **Sarf:** how important words are formed from root to form used in the passage
  * **Nahw notes** where helpful
* **Tracks passage number** and gives a correctness summary after every 10 passages
* Real-life situations preferred (avoid poetic or imaginary scenarios)

---
#### Learning priority
- Sarf (word formation): ~60%
- Nahw (sentence grammar): ~25%
- Listening/speaking/input: ~15%

#### Dialect policy
Najdi is always primary. Brief MSA / Najdi / Hijazi / Gulf / Iraqi / Levantine / Egyptian comparison when relevant. Do not deeply drill dialect differences unless explicitly requested.

---
#### Verb & root tracking
**Tracker file:** [verb-root-tracker.md](verb-root-tracker.md)

- Add every **new verb** and **new root** the first time it appears (drills, passages, chat, listening).
- Update coverage counts; report after each session: "Coverage: X roots, Y verbs encountered (Z drilled)."
- **`drill verb`** marks that verb as fully drilled.
- **`coverage`** or **`stats`** returns current counts.
