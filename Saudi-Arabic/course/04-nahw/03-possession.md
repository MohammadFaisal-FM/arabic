# Lesson 4.3 — Possession

## Ways to show "my / your / his"

### 1. Attached pronoun (idāfa-style suffix)

| English | Arabic | Breakdown |
|---------|--------|-----------|
| my house | بيت**ي** | bayt + ī |
| your house (m.) | بيت**ك** | bayt + ka |
| his house | بيت**ه** | bayt + hu |
| her house | بيت**ها** | bayt + hā |
| our house | بيت**نا** | bayt + nā |

### 2. Idāfa (construct) — "X of Y"

| Arabic | English |
|--------|---------|
| بيت **ال**ولد | the boy's house / house of the boy |
| كتاب **ال**معلم | the teacher's book |

### 3. Preposition **ل** (l-) — "belongs to"

| Arabic | English |
|--------|---------|
| الكتاب **لي** | the book is mine |
| **ل**جدتي | for / belonging to my grandmother |
