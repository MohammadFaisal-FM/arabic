# Lesson 4.5 — Agreement (Gender & Number)

## Gender agreement

Adjectives and verbs often **match** the subject's gender:

| Arabic | English |
|--------|---------|
| الولد **كبير** | the boy is big (m.) |
| البنت **كبيرة** | the girl is big (f. — extra ة) |
| هو **راح** | he went |
| هي **راحت** | she went |

---

## Number agreement

| Number | Verb example (went) |
|--------|---------------------|
| he | **راح** |
| she | **راحت** |
| they (m.) | **راحوا** |
| we | **رحنا** |

---

## English speaker tip

English: "they went" — one form.  
Arabic: verb ending changes for **gender + number**. This is why conjugation tables matter.
