# الضمائر · Damāʾir (Pronouns)

**Damāʾir** (ضمائر) = pronouns. In Arabic grammar they are a type of **ism** (noun) — they name a person without naming them with a full noun.

This page matches how the app drills verbs: **8 everyday pronouns** in Saudi / Najdi speech, plus the full **Fusha** picture and **suffixes** on words like **كتابي** / **كتابك**.

---

## 1. How many in Fusha / classical Arabic?

Arabic does not use one tiny list like English *I, you, he, she, we, they*. Classical **Fusha** (فصحى) is more precise:

| What | Count | Arabic term |
|------|------:|-------------|
| **Independent** pronouns (stand alone) | **12** | ضمائر منفصلة |
| **Attached** pronouns (suffixes on nouns/verbs) | **9** on nouns (more on verbs) | ضمائر متصلة |

### Independent pronouns — full Fusha (12)

These can appear as the subject of a sentence by themselves.

| Arabic | Transliteration | English |
|--------|----------------|---------|
| أنا | anā | I |
| نحن | naḥnu | we |
| أنت | anta | you (m.) |
| أنتِ | anti | you (f.) |
| أنتما | antumā | you two |
| أنتم | antum | you (m. pl.) |
| أنتن | antunna | you (f. pl.) |
| هو | huwa | he |
| هي | hiya | she |
| هما | humā | they two |
| هم | hum | they (m.) |
| هن | hunna | they (f.) |

### Examples (Fusha / MSA)

| Arabic | English |
|--------|---------|
| **أنا** طالب | I am a student |
| **نحن** طلاب | We are students |
| **أنت** معلم | You (m.) are a teacher |
| **أنتِ** معلمة | You (f.) are a teacher |
| **هو** في البيت | He is in the house |
| **هي** في المدرسة | She is at school |
| **هم** في السوق | They are at the market |

Dual forms (**أنتما**, **هما**) are correct in Fusha but rare in everyday Saudi talk.

## 2. The 8 pronouns in this app (boiled down)

For **Fiʿl** conjugation drills and daily Najdi speech, we use **8** — not all 12.

| # | Arabic | English | Why this one? |
|---|--------|---------|----------------|
| 1 | أنا | I | Same as Fusha |
| 2 | إحنا | we | Najdi everyday (**نحن** → **إحنا** / **احنا**) |
| 3 | أنت | you (m.) | Same as Fusha |
| 4 | أنتِ | you (f.) | Same as Fusha |
| 5 | أنتم | you (pl.) | One “you all” bucket in speech |
| 6 | هو | he | Same as Fusha |
| 7 | هي | she | Same as Fusha |
| 8 | هم | they | Covers **هم** + **هن** in real talk |

### What we skip (for now)

| Fusha form | English | Why skip in beginner Najdi set |
|------------|---------|--------------------------------|
| نحن | we | Use **إحنا** in speech |
| أنتما | you two | Dual — rare |
| أنتن | you (f. pl.) | Merged into **أنتم** in conversation |
| هما | they two | Dual — rare |
| هن | they (f.) | Often same as **هم** in Najdi |

**Rule:** Learn the **8** first. Add duals and feminine-plural-only forms when you meet them in formal Arabic.

## 3. Suffixes — who the word refers to

English often uses separate words: *my book*, *your book*. Arabic often **sticks a short suffix** onto the noun or verb.

Think: **word + suffix = who it belongs to / who does it**

### Possession on **كتاب** (book)

| English | Arabic | Suffix | Breakdown |
|---------|--------|--------|-----------|
| my book | كتاب**ي** | **-ي** | kitāb + ī |
| your book (m.) | كتاب**ك** | **-ك** | kitāb + ka |
| your book (f.) | كتاب**ك** | **-ك** | same spelling; pronunciation may differ |
| his book | كتاب**ه** | **-ه** | kitāb + hu |
| her book | كتاب**ها** | **-ها** | kitāb + hā |
| our book | كتاب**نا** | **-نا** | kitāb + nā |
| your book (pl.) | كتاب**كم** | **-كم** | kitāb + kum |
| their book | كتاب**هم** | **-هم** | kitāb + hum |

### More everyday examples

| Arabic | Transliteration | English |
|--------|----------------|---------|
| بيت**ي** | baytī | my house |
| أم**ي** | ummī | my mother |
| جوال**ك** | jawwālak | your phone |
| أهل**نا** | ahlna | our family |
| عيال**هم** | iyālhum | their kids |

### Suffixes on **verbs** (who did the action)

Same idea on the **verb** — especially **past tense**:

| Pronoun | Past example (root ك-ت-ب) | English |
|---------|----------------------------|---------|
| أنا | كتب**ت** | I wrote |
| إحنا | كتب**نا** | we wrote |
| أنت | كتب**ت** | you (m.) wrote |
| أنتِ | كتب**تِ** | you (f.) wrote |
| أنتم | كتب**توا** | you (pl.) wrote |
| هو | كتب | he wrote |
| هي | كتب**ت** | she wrote |
| هم | كتب**وا** | they wrote |

**Pattern:** the verb root stays; the **ending** tells you **who**.
