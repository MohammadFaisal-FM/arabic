# Lesson 2.4 — Particles (حرف / Harf)

## English → Arabic

| English category | Usually Arabic |
|------------------|----------------|
| Preposition (in, on, from) | **حرف** |
| Conjunction (and, but) | **حرف** |
| Negation (not, no) | **حرف** |
| Question particles | **حرف** |
| Future prefix (will) | **حرف** or prefix |

**Harf** = word that **needs another word** to complete meaning. Alone, it feels incomplete.

---

## Common harf list

| Arabic | Transliteration | English role |
|--------|-----------------|--------------|
| في | fī | in |
| على | ala | on |
| من | min | from |
| إلى | ila | to |
| و | wa | and |
| أو | aw | or |
| لا | lā | no / not |
| ما | mā | not / what (context) |
| س | sa- | will (MSA future) |
| ب | bi- | with / by (Najdi future on verbs: **ب**جي) |

---

## Common combinations (Najdi)

| Combo | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| فيه | in it / there is |
| عليه | on him / his problem |
| منه | from him |
| لك | for you / you have |
