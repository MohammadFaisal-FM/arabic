# Lesson 3.1 — What Is a Root?

## English vs Arabic word building

| English | Arabic |
|---------|--------|
| write → wrote → written (irregular) | **ك-ت-ب** → كتب، يكتب، مكتوب، كاتب، كتاب |
| eat → ate → eaten | **أ-ك-ل** → أكل، يأكل، آكل، مأكول |

Most Arabic words come from **3-letter roots** (sometimes 4).

---

## Root chain (memorize this order)

```
ROOT
  ↓
COMMON FORM(S) / verb pattern
  ↓
VERB (past & present)
  ↓
MASDAR (verbal noun — "the act of …")
  ↓
ACTIVE PARTICIPLE (doer / doing)
  ↓
PASSIVE PARTICIPLE (done / undergone)
  ↓
DERIVED NOUNS
```

---

## Example: ك-ت-ب

| Form | Arabic | English |
|------|--------|---------|
| Root | ك-ت-ب | writing |
| Past verb | كتب | he wrote |
| Present | يكتب | he writes |
| Masdar | كتابة | writing |
| Active participle | كاتب | writer |
| Passive participle | مكتوب | written |
| Noun | كتاب | book |
