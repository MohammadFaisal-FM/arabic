# Lesson 6.1 — MSA to Najdi: What Changes

## Same grammar, different surface

| Layer | MSA | Najdi |
|-------|-----|-------|
| Roots & Sarf | ✓ same | ✓ same |
| Nahw basics | ✓ same | ✓ same |
| Everyday words | formal | local |
| Future tense | س + verb | **ب** + verb |
| Some verbs | يأكل | **ياكل** / **yakul** |
| Family word | عائلة | **أهل** |

---

## Common swaps

| MSA | Najdi | English |
|-----|-------|---------|
| كيف حالك | شلونك | how are you |
| ماذا | وش / إيش | what |
| أين | وين | where |
| جيد | زين | good |
| الآن | الحين | now |
| سآتي | بجي | I will come |
| سأفعل | بسوي | I will do |

---

## What NOT to worry about yet

- Full iʿrāb (case endings)
- Dual forms in speech
- All 10 verb forms

---

2. كيف حالكم → **شلونكم**  
3. ماذا تفعل → **وش تسوي**
