## Drill root: ك-ت-ب

**Root:** ك-ت-ب — writing

| Form | Arabic | English |
|------|--------|---------|
| Past | كتب | he wrote |
| Present | يكتب | he writes |
| Masdar | كتابة | writing |
| Active participle | كاتب | writer |
| Passive participle | مكتوب | written |
| Noun | كتاب | book |

**Example:** كتبت رسالة لجدي — *I wrote a letter to my grandfather.*

```text
drill root ك-ت-ب
```

## Drill root: أ-ك-ل

**Root:** أ-ك-ل — eating

| Form | Arabic | English |
|------|--------|---------|
| Past | أكل | he ate |
| Present | ياكل | he eats |
| Masdar | أكل | eating |
| Active participle | آكل | eating / eater |
| Passive participle | مأكول | eaten / food |

**Example:** أكلنا غدا عند جدتي — *We ate lunch at grandma's.*

```text
drill root أ-ك-ل
```
