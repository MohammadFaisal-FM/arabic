# نزل

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Type** | فعل · fiʿl (verb) |
| **Arabic** | نزل |
| **Present** | ينزل |
| **Meaning** | going down / getting off |
| **Root** | [ن-ز-ل](#roots/nzl) |

## Example

| Arabic · English |
|------------------|
| نزلت من السيارة — I got out of the car |
