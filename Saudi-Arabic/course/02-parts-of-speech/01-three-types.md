# Lesson 2.1 — The Three Word Types

## English vs Arabic classification

| English grammar term | Arabic term | Arabic script | Symbol |
|---------------------|-------------|---------------|--------|
| Noun, adjective, pronoun, adverb (often) | **Noun / name word** | **اسم** | ism |
| Verb | **Verb / action word** | **فعل** | fi'l |
| Preposition, conjunction, negation, etc. | **Particle** | **حرف** | harf |

**Rule:** Every Arabic word is **exactly one** of these three.

---

## Why only three?

Arabic grammar groups by **function in sentence**, not like English (8 parts of speech). An "adjective" in English is usually an **ism** in Arabic (e.g. كبير = big).

---

## Quick classify

| Word | Type | Why |
|------|------|-----|
| بيت | ism | names a thing |
| كتب | fi'l | action |
| في | harf | links words, no full meaning alone |
| كبير | ism | describes (adjective = ism) |
| لا | harf | negation particle |
| أنا | ism | pronoun = ism |

---

## Command: drill word

When you learn any word, type:

```text
drill word [word]
```

The tutor classifies it and applies the right template (A = root family, B = ism, C = harf).
