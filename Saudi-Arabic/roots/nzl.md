# ن-ز-ل

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Root** | ن-ز-ل |
| **Letter** | ن · نون |
| **Meaning** | going down / getting off |
| **Form I** | نزل / ينزل |
| **Dialect** | Everyday Saudi / Najdi + MSA where common |

---

## Example

| Arabic · English |
|------------------|
| نزلت من السيارة — I got out of the car |
