# Lesson 1.1 — Alphabet & Direction

## Arabic script basics

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Direction** | Right to left ← |
| **Letters** | 28 letters |
| **Vowels** | Short vowels (َ ِ ُ) often omitted in writing |
| **No capitals** | Same letter shapes everywhere |

---

## The 28 letters (names)

ا ب ت ث ج ح خ د ذ ر ز س ش ص ض ط ظ ع غ ف ق ك ل م ن ه و ي

**Grouped for English speakers:**

| Group | Letters | Rough English sound |
|-------|---------|---------------------|
| Similar to English | ب ت ث ج د ذ ر ز س ف ك ل م ن ه و ي | b, t, th, j, d, dh, r, z, s, f, k, l, m, n, h, w, y |
| Emphatic (heavy) | ص ض ط ظ | deeper t, d — learn by listening |
| Throat letters | ح خ ع غ | no English equivalent — practice slowly |
| Special | ا ق | alif (glide/a), qaf (deep k) |

---

## Connecting shapes

Most letters change shape depending on position:

| Position | Example (ب) |
|----------|-------------|
| Alone | ب |
| Start of word | بـ |
| Middle | ـبـ |
| End | ـب |

**Non-connectors** (only one shape): ا د ذ ر ز و

---

## First word to read

**بيت** (bayt) = house

Letters: ب + ي + ت — read right to left.

---

## Practice

1. Write your name in Arabic letters (transliteration is fine)
2. Read **بيت**, **كتب**, **أكل** aloud
3. Use Duolingo Arabic or similar for **script speed** (MSA script = same letters)
