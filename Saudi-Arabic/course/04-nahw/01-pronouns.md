# Lesson 4.1 — Pronouns

## Independent pronouns

| Arabic | Transliteration | English |
|--------|-----------------|---------|
| أنا | anā | I |
| إحنا / احنا | iḥna | we (Najdi) |
| أنت | anta | you (m.) |
| أنتِ | anti | you (f.) |
| أنتم / antu | antum / antu | you (pl.) |
| هو | huwa | he |
| هي | hiya | she |
| هم | hum | they |

---

## Attached pronouns (suffixes)

English uses separate words ("my house"). Arabic often **attaches** to the noun/verb:

| English | Example | Arabic |
|---------|---------|--------|
| my book | book-my | كتاب**ي** |
| his house | house-his | بيت**ه** |
| she asked **me** | asked-me | سأل**ت****ني** |

---

## Najdi pronoun note

Najdi often uses **احنا** (ihna) and drops some case endings you see in MSA textbooks.

## Exercise

Make 3 sentences:

1. I + in the house → **أنا في البيت**
2. she + ate → **هي أكلت**
3. we + will come → **إحنا بجي** / **احنا بنجي**
